# Colour research and how the Moon theme was chosen (2–3 Nov 2026)

## 1. Live research: 20 agency homepages (2 Nov)
Screenshots were taken live in a browser and are in `previews/agency-colors-international.jpg` and `previews/agency-colors-india.jpg`.

- **International (10):** Ogilvy, AKQA, Huge, R/GA, Wieden+Kennedy, Monks, WebFX, NP Digital, Jellyfish, Accenture Song.
- **Indian (10):** Schbang, WATConsult, Infidigit, PageTraffic, Social Beat, Gozoop, Lyxel&Flamingo, Ethinos, Webenza, BC Web Wise.
- Social Panga and Kinnect did not load (certificate error), and SoCheers' domain is for sale, so those three were replaced.

**Common factors found:**
1. 85–90% of the screen is neutral (black, white or charcoal). Colour fills only 5–10%.
2. Usually one accent colour, used on the logo, buttons and links.
3. Almost no gradients; premium comes from big bold type.
4. International sites lean dark (7 of 10 have a black base). Indian sites lean light (6 of 10 have a white base).
5. Only 3 of 20 use blue (WebFX, WATConsult, Ethinos), all SEO or tech type agencies.

| 2–3 Nov | R1 Royal Sky, R2 Royal + Coral, R3 Royal + Mint, R4 Royal + Gold, R5 Royal Multi | asked to change R3 mint to yellow |

| 3 Nov | **Moon theme: moon white + dark blue + dark yellow** (reference: WildNet Technologies homepage, white page, dark navy blocks, yellow-orange accents) | **Final.** Then added more icons and yellow key words in headings (like the reference). |

## 3. Final (see DESIGN.md for every token)
- Moon white `#F5F7FA`
- Dark blue `#13245E` and `#0C1845`
- Dark yellow `#F2B200`, with `#FFC93C` on dark and mustard `#8A6500` as text on white
- Yellow gradient buttons `#FFE07A` → `#F2B200` → `#D99A00`
