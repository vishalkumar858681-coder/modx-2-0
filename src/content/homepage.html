Choosing an agency

What does a digital marketing agency do?

A digital marketing agency plans and runs online channels that bring customers to a business: SEO, paid ads, content, social media, email and website work. Modexa Digital covers SEO, AEO, GEO, performance marketing, ecommerce marketing, web design and development, software and web apps, content writing, video editing and guest posting.

Are digital marketing agencies worth it?

They are worth it when you need skills, tools and time that you do not have in-house, and when the agency ties its work to leads and sales. They are not worth it if the agency only reports rankings and clicks, or promises results nobody can control. Ask for a clear plan, named people and monthly reports.

How do I choose a digital marketing agency in India?

Check five things: proof of past work with real numbers, who will actually do the work, how they report, what the contract says, and whether they can explain their plan in plain words. Ask them to audit one of your pages before you sign. Avoid agencies that guarantee position 1.

Digital marketing agency vs in-house team: which is better?

An in-house team knows your business best but is limited to the skills you hire. An agency brings several specialists at once, usually at lower cost than hiring each one. Many companies keep one marketing owner in-house and use an agency for SEO, ads and web work.

Digital marketing agency vs freelancer: which is better?

A freelancer fits a single, well-defined task. An agency fits work that needs several skills together, such as SEO, content, ads and a website. The main trade-offs are cost, speed, backup when someone is unavailable and how well the channels work together.

AI and the future of search

What are AI SEO services?

AI SEO services help your brand show up in AI-powered search: Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot. They include answer engine optimization (AEO), generative engine optimization (GEO), schema markup, entity SEO and AI visibility audits.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO earns rankings in Google's results. AEO earns the short answer shown in AI Overviews, featured snippets and voice search. GEO earns mentions and citations inside AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. They share the same base of clear content, technical health and trusted brand signals, so we plan them together.

Will SEO be replaced by AI?

No. SEO is changing, not disappearing. AI search tools still draw on crawlable, trusted, well-structured content, so the work moves toward clear answers, strong entities, third-party mentions and technical health, on top of classic SEO.

Will SEO still work in 5 years?

Nobody can predict the exact shape of search, but the core of SEO, being the clearest and most trusted answer to a real question, is likely to stay useful. We build content and brand signals that work for Google today and for AI assistants as they grow.

How do I get my business mentioned in ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?

State clearly who you are, what you offer and who it is for. Use the same facts on your site, Google Business Profile and trusted directories, add schema markup, answer real customer questions on your pages and earn mentions on reputable third-party sites. We audit where you appear today and fix the gaps.

Performance marketing

What is a performance marketing agency?

A performance marketing agency runs paid advertising and judges it by measurable results such as leads, sales, cost per lead and return on ad spend (ROAS), not impressions. Modexa Digital manages Google Ads, Performance Max, Meta Ads and remarketing, with conversion tracking checked before spend starts.

Is performance marketing worth it?

It is worth it when your offer, website and tracking are ready, because ads then bring measurable leads or sales. It is a poor fit if your site does not convert or tracking is broken. We check both in the audit.

What is the difference between digital marketing and performance marketing?

Digital marketing is the whole set of online channels, including SEO, content, social and email. Performance marketing is the paid part, where you pay for measured results such as clicks, leads or sales. Most businesses do best when both work from one plan.

Cost

How much does a digital marketing agency charge in India?

Price depends on scope: which services you need, how many pages or campaigns are involved, and your market. Agencies usually charge a monthly retainer, a project fee, or a share of ad spend. Modexa Digital plans start at [₹X per month]; the free audit gives you a fixed quote for your scope.

How much do digital marketing agencies charge per month?

Monthly fees rise with the number of channels, the size of the site and how much content or ad management is needed. Ask what is included, how many hours or deliverables you get and who does the work, not only the headline price.

How much do SEO services cost in India?

SEO cost depends on site size, competition, content volume and whether technical, content and link work are all needed. A small local business needs far less than a large ecommerce site. We scope SEO after an audit so you pay for work your site needs.

How much do performance marketing agencies charge?

Common models are a flat monthly management fee, a percentage of ad spend, or a mix. Your ad budget is paid to the platforms, such as Google and Meta, and is separate from the agency fee. Ask how the agency reports ROAS and cost per lead.

SEO

What do SEO services include?

SEO services usually include a site audit, keyword and topic research, technical SEO fixes, on-page SEO (titles, content, internal links, schema), off-page SEO (links and mentions), local SEO where relevant, and monthly reporting.

Are SEO services worth it?

They are worth it when your buyers search before they decide, because organic traffic keeps working after you stop paying per click. The return builds over months, so SEO fits best as a steady investment, often next to paid ads for faster results.

How do SEO agencies work?

A good SEO agency audits your site, finds what holds back crawling and rankings, plans content around real searches, fixes technical issues, builds trusted mentions and links, and reports progress against agreed goals.

What are local SEO services?

Local SEO helps a business show up in Google Maps and "near me" searches. It covers Google Business Profile optimization, consistent name, address and phone details across directories, location pages and review management.

What is technical SEO, and what is the difference between on-page and off-page SEO?

Technical SEO lets search engines crawl, render and index your site correctly, including speed, sitemaps, canonicals and structured data. On-page SEO is what you control on your pages: content, titles, headings and internal links. Off-page SEO is the trust signals from other sites, mainly links and brand mentions.
